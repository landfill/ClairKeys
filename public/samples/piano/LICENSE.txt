mp3 conversion used as the source here: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/
(Tone.js project, MIT-licensed code, redistributing the CC-BY samples.)

These files are MODIFIED from the source, as CC-BY 3.0 permits and requires to be
stated. `scripts/build-piano-samples.sh` in this repository performs and records
the modifications:

  - folded from stereo to mono
  - trimmed per register (6.0s below C3, 4.0s to A5, 2.0s above) with a 0.5s
    fade at the trim point
  - re-encoded to 80 kbps mp3
  - renamed from note names to MIDI note numbers

No change was made to the relative levels between samples: the difference between
registers is the instrument's own balance, and normalising each file separately
would destroy it.
